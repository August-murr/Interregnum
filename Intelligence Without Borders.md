# Intelligence Without Borders

### Representation beyond the US–China AI rivalry

*Written in collaboration with Sol.*

**Intelligence Without Borders (IWB)** is not a proposal for a single international AI governing body, nor is it an organization I am currently planning to found. It is an idea about something that may be necessary: organized representation for countries outside the United States and China as AI becomes more powerful.

Much of the future of AI is currently discussed through the interests of its two leading powers. This risks reducing a global transformation to a national competition in which every other country is expected to accept decisions made elsewhere.

IWB would push against the nationalist idea that the country that develops advanced AI should isolate it and keep its benefits for itself. AI may be developed within national borders, but its consequences—and potentially its benefits—will not remain within them.

The central purpose would be to defend the interests and rights of countries that otherwise have little influence over AI development and policy. This could include pooling resources from multiple countries to support shared research, coordinating advocacy or lobbying, and developing positions that challenge attempts to monopolize AI capabilities and their benefits.

The core idea is not simply “more international governance.” It is that the rest of the world needs the ability to act together rather than being represented indirectly through either the United States or China. Without that representation, AI risks becoming another source of dependence and concentrated power instead of a technology that benefits humanity more broadly.
